# Earkeys

**Dictation practice for macOS. Catch the words your ear skips.**

Press <kbd>⌥ Space</kbd> over any app, listen to one English sentence, type what you heard, and see word by word what
your ear skipped. Earkeys lives in your menu bar, works offline, needs no account, and keeps your decks and review
history on your Mac.

## Download

**[Download the latest Earkeys.dmg](https://github.com/vanchonlee/earkeys-releases/releases/latest)**

Open the DMG and drag Earkeys to Applications. Builds are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS
opens them without warnings. Requires macOS 14 or later.

Each release lists its SHA-256 checksum. To check a download:

```bash
shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Earkeys-*.dmg
```
